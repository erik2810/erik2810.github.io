Updated July 2026, from Leipzig.

**Looking for my next role.** My contract at the Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the
Sciences ended in May 2026, the last two months of it as a Scientist. I am now looking for
fully remote ML engineering, R&D, or quantitative research positions in the EU. If you are
hiring, the [CV](/cv/) and [projects](/projects/) pages are the quickest overview, and
[email](mailto:eriklfholz@googlemail.com) reaches me directly.

**Finishing a paper.** An article on interacting vertex operators on the Euclidean AdS₂
background is in preparation for JHEP, together with my former MPI group. The
[research page](/research/) has the abstract.

**Building differentiable simulators.** The current cycle of side projects sits where my
physics background meets ML engineering: a [differentiable digital twin](https://github.com/erik2810/differentiable-digital-twin)
that optimises structures by backpropagating through a FEM solver, a
[JAX spring-physics engine](https://github.com/erik2810/jax-spring-sim), and a
[WebGPU cloth simulation](https://erik2810.github.io/webgpu-spring-mesh/) that runs in the
browser. Each one has a live demo, measured benchmarks, and a write-up on the [blog](/blog/).

**Training models on the side.** I work freelance with Outlier.ai on evaluation and training
data for coding models, which keeps me close to how large models actually fail.
